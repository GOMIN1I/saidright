**Saidright 1.2** · Apple silicon · macOS 14 or later · free and open source (Apache 2.0)

Not notarized by Apple. On first launch open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. The DMG's SHA-256 is attached as `Saidright.dmg.sha256`.

### Instant insert
Turn on **Settings → Text & AI → Insert instantly**: your words appear the moment you stop speaking, and the edited version replaces them a moment later. Saidright only replaces when you have not typed or clicked in between and the text is still where it was put; the replacement goes to that app alone. Otherwise the unedited text stays and the edited version is in the result.

### Fix it in one click
When a tip corrects your English (or any language you are learning), its card now has **Replace**: the corrected version goes straight into the text you just inserted. Prefer no clicks? **Settings → Learning → Fixes from tips → Automatically**. Only the wrong words change — uncertain and style tips never do.

### Real tables where they belong
Ask for a table by voice and Notes, Pages, Keynote, Word, Outlook, OneNote, Mail and TextEdit now receive a real table with borders, and lists arrive as real lists. Notion, Obsidian and Bear get Markdown; messengers and AI chats get clean text.

### Also in 1.1
Deep indigo dark theme, tables from any wording, lists that fit the app, faster long dictations, and Groq (`openai/gpt-oss-120b`) as the recommended text service for new installs.

### Known limitations
Apple silicon only; most testing on the newest macOS. Instant insert and Replace need a field that shows its text to Accessibility (most Mac apps and browser text boxes); in editors that hide it the unedited text stays. Free cloud tiers have per-minute and daily limits. Full list: docs/TESTING.md.
