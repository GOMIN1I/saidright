**Saidright 1.1** · Apple silicon · macOS 14 or later · free and open source (Apache 2.0)

Not notarized by Apple. On first launch open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. The DMG's SHA-256 is attached as `Saidright.dmg.sha256`.

### Deep indigo at night
The dark theme now uses the product page's colours — night indigo, voice blue and a faint coral — and fills the whole window, including the band behind the close, minimise and zoom buttons.

### Tables from your own words
Ask for a table however you like — “make a comparison table of ChatGPT and Claude”, columns without values, rows by name, “compare by price, quality, speed”. Every cell still comes from what you said.

### Lists that fit the app
Notes apps, code editors and terminals get Markdown. Messengers, mail, browsers and AI chats get plain text: tasks and steps numbered 1. 2. 3., other lists with •, no checkbox brackets. Tables stay tables.

### Faster
- Long dictations: recognition ahead also uses shorter pauses once 12 s of speech are waiting — on long real recordings the wait after you stop went from 2.1 s to 0.8 s on average (worst case 6.4 s → 1.3 s).
- Rewriting lays out lists in the same request, so long dictations need one text request instead of two.

### Groq by default for new installs
Settings → Text & AI now lists **Groq (recommended)** first with `openai/gpt-oss-120b` — about 0.4 s per short phrase in our tests and it passed every faithfulness check (no translation between your languages, learner errors kept, no instructions followed from the dictation). Create a free key at console.groq.com and paste it in. OpenAI, a local Ollama model and any OpenAI-compatible server remain available; existing settings are not changed.

### Known limitations
Apple silicon only; most testing on the newest macOS. Free cloud tiers have per-minute and daily limits; when a request is refused, Saidright inserts the recognised text unedited. Full list: docs/TESTING.md.
